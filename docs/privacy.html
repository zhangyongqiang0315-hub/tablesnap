Privacy Policy for TableSnap

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

1. Data Collection

TableSnap runs 100% locally inside your browser. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any webpage content, extracted tables, browsing history, or personal data.

2. Permissions Used

  • activeTab & scripting: Used strictly when you click the extension icon to scan tables on the active tab.
  • storage: Used locally to remember your Pro activation status.
  • api.gumroad.com: Contacted only when you enter a license key to verify your Pro purchase.

3. Contact

For support or privacy questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
